Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Personal Encrypted Backup handles Google user data.

Data accessed

After the user grants permission, the utility can access the user's Google Drive to create, list, read, update, and delete objects that form the encrypted backup repository.

Purpose of access

Google Drive access is used solely for user-directed backup, incremental snapshot storage, integrity verification, retention maintenance, and restoration.

Storage and encryption

Backup contents are encrypted on the user's computer before they are uploaded to Google Drive. Authentication credentials are stored locally on the user's computer and are not published on this site. The utility has no separate application server or operator-controlled cloud database.

Sharing, transfer, and sale

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers, or shared with third parties. Data remains under the user's control in the user's own Google Drive account, except where Google processes it as the Drive service provider.

Retention and deletion

Encrypted backup objects remain in the user's Google Drive until removed by the user's configured retention process or deleted by the user. The user can delete the backup repository from Google Drive and revoke the utility's access from Google Account security settings at any time.

Limited use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the owner through the public information repository.

Effective date: October 2, 2026
